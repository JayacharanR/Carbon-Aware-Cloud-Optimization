Carbon-Aware Placement with a Fail-Closed Gate

An untrusted proposer, here Llama-3 8B, may suggest a placement. Gate v2.1 admits a proposal only if it is feasible on the scheduler's own model, within 5% of the carbon-optimal choice, and made on carbon data that pass age and identity checks. Otherwise the scheduler uses the optimal placement, or holds.
